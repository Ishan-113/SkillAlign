About SkillAlign

Smart India Hackathon 2026 - Problem Statement 26134

Problem Statement

PS 26134 focuses on Skill Development & Industry Alignment for the Government of Maharashtra. The challenge is to bridge the gap between what educational institutions teach and what industries actually require.

Our Solution

SkillAlign analyzes real job market data to identify skill gaps, experience requirements, and geographic distribution of opportunities. This enables targeted skill development programs and better career guidance.
